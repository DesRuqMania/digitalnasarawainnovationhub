Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Nasarawa Innovation Hub collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website, read our content, or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly when you contact us, subscribe for updates, fill out a form, request information, or participate in our events, programs, or communications. This may include your name, email address, phone number, organization, and message details.

We may also collect non-personally identifiable information automatically through standard website analytics and advertising tools. This may include browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, time spent on site, referral source, and interaction patterns. This information helps us understand how the website is used and improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate our website, respond to inquiries, improve user experience, support events and training programs, communicate updates, and maintain the effectiveness of our digital outreach and learning initiatives.

We may also use information to understand general website traffic and engagement, identify popular content, protect our platform from misuse, and support our organizational communication and digital strategy.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember user preferences, improve site function, analyze traffic, and support advertising. Cookies can help us understand which pages are most useful, how visitors move through the site, and whether certain content or campaigns are more effective.

Cookies may be used for essential website functionality, analytics, user experience improvements, and advertising-related purposes. Some cookies are session-based and expire when you close your browser, while others may remain for a longer period to remember preferences.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookie settings. You can usually choose to accept, reject, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may limit some website features or reduce the personalization of your experience.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Our website may use advertising services, including Google AdSense, to support free access to content. These advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to display relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and understand user interaction with the ads shown on the site.

We do not sell your personal information. However, third-party advertising networks and analytics providers may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those providers for more information about how they handle data.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to comply with legal obligations, or to support legitimate organizational needs.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may choose not to provide some information when requested, although this may limit certain features or services. If you contact us and request access, correction, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you, we will consider your request in line with applicable law and our operational needs.

You can also manage your browser settings to reject or delete cookies and limit tracking. If you want to stop receiving email updates from us, you can unsubscribe using the link in our messages or contact us directly.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent or as required by applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information should contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, partner organizations, social media pages, or other digital resources. These external sites are not controlled by us and may have their own privacy policies and data practices. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third-party sites.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, legal requirements, or technology. Any updates will be posted on this page and the date at the bottom of the policy will be revised accordingly.

Your continued use of the website after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
