Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Digital Nasarawa Innovation Hub collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website, read our content, or engage with our community.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, or other contact details when you contact us, subscribe to updates, or submit an inquiry. We may also collect non-personal technical information automatically, including your browser type, device type, IP address, operating system, referring pages, time of visit, and general usage patterns.

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, improve site functionality, understand visitor behavior, and support advertising. Cookies may include information about browsing activity and ad interactions.

2. How we use your information

We use data to operate and improve the website, respond to inquiries, provide relevant updates, analyze traffic patterns, improve user experience, and support our outreach and program communication. We may also use website data to understand which content is most useful to visitors and to deliver appropriate advertising in line with our third-party ad partners.

3. Cookies and tracking technologies

Cookies help us understand how visitors use our website and allow us to personalize or improve content. We may use cookies for essential site operations, analytics, advertising, and user preference management.

You can disable or manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality. Some third-party advertising partners may also use cookies to deliver personalized ads or measure ad performance.

4. Advertising and third-party services

Our website may use advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to display relevant ads. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to measure ad performance, provide personalized advertisements, and understand user interactions with advertisements. Ad serving is subject to the privacy policies of the relevant providers.

We do not sell personal information. However, third-party advertising networks may process information collected on or through our website in accordance with their own privacy practices.

5. Data retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or to comply with legal obligations. We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Your rights and choices

You may choose not to provide some information, although this may limit access to certain features. If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data that you have provided, please contact us using the details below. You may also opt out of cookie-based advertising or adjust browser settings to control cookie usage.

7. Children's privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13 for collection of personal information without parental consent. We encourage parents and guardians to monitor online activity and contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent.

8. Links to external sites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or social media pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. Please review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the website after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
